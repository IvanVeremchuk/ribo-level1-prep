# RIBO Level 1 Self-Study

A free study app for the **RIBO Level 1 (Entry-Level Broker) exam**, the licensing exam for general insurance brokers in Ontario, Canada.

- 8 short lessons and a glossary of abbreviations
- 490 original practice questions with explanations, by exam topic
- A timed 115-question mock exam in the real exam's proportions
- About 7 hours of audio: lessons, memory drills and every question read aloud
- Progress memory: which questions you've seen and missed, a "review my mistakes" mode, mock exam history and a daily study log, saved on your device with backup and restore
- Works offline and installs on your phone like an app

## Use it

Open the site on your phone, then:
- **Android (Chrome):** menu (⋮) → Install app / Add to Home screen
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → Add to Home Screen

## Important

- This project is **not affiliated with or endorsed by RIBO** (the Registered Insurance Brokers of Ontario), IBAO or the Insurance Institute.
- The practice questions are **original**. They follow RIBO's published 2025 competency profile and blueprint, but they are not real exam questions. RIBO keeps its exam questions confidential. The only real past questions are in RIBO's free sample questions document.
- Audio narration is AI-generated.
- Rules change. Ontario auto accident benefits changed on July 1, 2026. Always check facts against official sources: [RIBO](https://www.ribo.com/getting-a-license/individual-licenses/new-applicants/examinations/level-1-entry-level-broker-exam/) and [FSRA](https://www.fsrao.ca/).
